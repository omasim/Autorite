# DECISION-0001 — Two-Site Launch Architecture
**Date:** 2026-10-07  
**Status:** ACTIVE

## Decision
Launch two separate websites from the beginning:
- autorite.org — public knowledge/publication;
- autorite.net — live research laboratory/network.

Both are independent applications/deployments but project the same canonical Git research records.

## Rejected
A full autorite.net redirect and two independent content stores.

## Rationale
The two domains serve materially different audiences and interaction modes. Separation is useful at launch, while a shared canonical research source prevents epistemic divergence.

## Revisit if
Operating two applications creates disproportionate maintenance cost, or future organizational/security boundaries require separate repositories.
