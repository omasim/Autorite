# DECISION-0002 — Horizon Identity and Cycle Ring
**Date:** 2026-10-07  
**Status:** ACTIVE

## Horizon mark
Autorite's selected identity direction is **Horizon**:
- an open/incomplete circular arc;
- a restrained horizon curve beneath it;
- monochrome-first construction;
- no letterform required in the symbol.

Meaning: open inquiry, an unfinished horizon of knowledge, continuity without a claim of final closure.

## Cycle Ring
The Cycle Ring is a separate but related system:
- perfect circular progress ring;
- Cycle number centered inside (`01`, `02`, ...);
- ring fill represents research-obligation completion, not elapsed time;
- unfilled segment represents outstanding Cycle obligations;
- 100% ring = `CLOSED`;
- after publication the closed ring remains historically visible.

## Semantic distinction
`Horizon Logo = Open Inquiry`  
`Cycle Ring = Finite Research Commitment`

Do not merge the logo and Cycle Ring into one mark. Their visual kinship is intentional; their meanings are distinct.

## Shared use
Both autorite.org and autorite.net use the same Horizon identity and Cycle Ring semantics. `.org` renders the ring minimally/editorially; `.net` may expose the obligation breakdown behind the same ring.
