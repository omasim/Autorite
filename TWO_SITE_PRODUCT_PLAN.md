# Two-Site Product Plan

## Shared institutional model
Two independent websites, one canonical research record.

## autorite.org — Knowledge / Publication
Audience: public, researchers, institutions.
Purpose: durable readable body of Autorite.
Routes: `/questions`, `/knowledge`, `/research`, `/reflections`, `/archive`, `/methods`, `/about`, `/changes`, `/publications`.
Claim pages expose statement, type, scope, status, evidence/counterevidence, assumptions, limits, `does not imply`, what could change it, genealogy and source RPs.
Character: editorial, spacious, calm, rigorous.

## autorite.net — Laboratory / Network
Audience: active researchers, reviewers, contributors, developers.
Purpose: expose research while it happens.
Routes: `/lab`, `/cycle/01`, `/rp/{id}`, `/experiments`, `/results`, `/reviews`, `/challenges`, `/legacy/damx`, `/code`, `/data`, `/releases`, `/contribute`, `/method-status`.
RP pages expose preregistration, collision, runs, results, deviations, diagnostics, decisions, blockers, commits/releases and claims produced.
Character: technical, dense, operational; not a generic SaaS dashboard.

## Cross-site contract
- Claim/Question IDs are shared.
- `.org` owns canonical public knowledge URLs.
- `.net` owns live operational research views.
- `.net` links canonical claims to `.org`.
- RP live page primary on `.net`; stable public summary may exist on `.org`.
- Publications/reflections primary on `.org`.
- Experiment logs/reproducibility primary on `.net`.
- Archive interpretation on `.org`; archaeology workbench on `.net`.
- Avoid duplicate full text; each domain has its own sitemap.
- Shared identity foundation, distinct navigation and information density.

## Shared Cycle Ring
Both sites use the same canonical Cycle completion value.
- Center: two-digit Cycle number.
- Ring: obligation-based completion.
- No default percentage numeral is required; the ring itself communicates progress.
- `autorite.org`: large, quiet editorial expression with Cycle title/status.
- `autorite.net`: operational expression; selecting the ring may reveal RP/review/synthesis/publication obligation breakdown.
- Closed Cycles retain a fully completed ring in the archive.

## Identity
Use the selected Horizon mark across both sites. Keep logo and Cycle Ring separate:
`Horizon = open-ended research`; `Cycle Ring = closable Cycle`.
