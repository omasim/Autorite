## Formal capability assessment

Existing mathematics represented the completed tasks: probabilistic prediction and representation maps in RP002A; feature equality, metric tolerance and path composition in RP003; branch genealogy and query decoding in RP004; finite DAG reachability and exact truncated reward planning in RP005A. No repeatedly demonstrated inability of these structures to express or answer a declared finite task has been established.

RP003's non-composing local tolerance is a property to represent, not evidence that metric mathematics must be replaced. RP004's inadequate projections are information omissions relative to queries, not a demonstrated deficit in directed genealogy structures. RP005A's policy losses are consequences of supplied rewards/admissibility and finite horizons, not failure of finite-graph reachability. RP002A's procedure/budget comparison does not prove that all existing representation mathematics is inadequate.

Retain the formal-capability contract and its weakest-sufficient-structure rule. Borrow existing mathematics first. A bridge proposal needs a specific representation or composition obligation and an adequacy comparison; new mathematics needs a recurring documented formal deficit. Current encodability does not prove optimality, minimality or adequacy outside these tasks. No new formal system or preferred ontology is introduced.
