## Method and source limits

RP006 independently replayed one authorized run over registered fixtures that RP004 had already introduced. Its exact classifications do not add new cases or independent empirical confirmation. The eight masks are whole-field ablations; they do not exhaust arbitrary encodings or establish a globally smallest representation. Preserve the registered query definitions, negative/conflict outcomes, UNKNOWN responses and incomparable masks when interpreting the finite result.

The five Results have unlike units and designs. Their counts cannot be pooled as a common sample or used to infer prevalence. Graph references establish traceability, not transitive support.
