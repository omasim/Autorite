## Assumptions and unresolved questions

Training units are treated as independent; episodes and timepoints are not extra training replicates. Actual-loss inference retains the frozen normal-theory assumptions. Synthetic calibration is not distribution-free or actual-loss coverage proof. The .01-nat margin is fixture-specific, not a universal substantive threshold. Capacity is approximately matched (579 versus 651 parameters), and the estimand is the fixed training algorithm, not an optimally trained model.

No generalization beyond the specified generator laws and parameters, no necessity of recurrent memory or retention of the entire history, no ontology or foundational theory, and no exhaustive novelty clearance follows. The broader Q-3 and Q-4 remain ACTIVE. External scientific review has not occurred; this is an explicit internal evidence and interpretation review.

They are open research directions, not silently added prerequisites for this already defined finite package.
